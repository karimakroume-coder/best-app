## Full Description

BEST answers the one question no platform can answer honestly: what is genuinely the best content in the world right now?

YouTube cannot objectively rank YouTube videos. TikTok cannot objectively rank TikTok videos. Every platform has a conflict of interest — they rank what makes THEM money, not what is genuinely best. BEST has no such conflict.

**INDEPENDENT RANKING**
BEST scores every video using a transparent algorithm built on four signals: velocity (how fast it's growing), retention (how much people actually like it), global reach (how many countries it touches), and elite discovery (whether top raters are watching). No platform can buy a higher rank. No advertiser can influence the score. The math is public. The independence is the entire point.

**THE SPATIAL MAP**
BEST is not a feed. It is a world. Every video has a permanent coordinate. The best content is always at the center. You navigate by swiping in four directions. Pinch to zoom between views. It feels like exploring, not scrolling.

**THE MARK**
When you see something that moves you, you can mark it — choose a color that captures how it made you feel, write one word that distills the moment, and a snapshot is taken. Your Mark lives on that video forever. Color is the language. One word is the anchor. This is how culture gets mapped.

**DISCOVERY SCORE**
Every action on BEST earns Discovery Score points — watching videos others haven't found yet, placing marks, fireflagging content you believe in. Higher scores unlock more influence in the ranking algorithm. The best discoverers become the best curators.

**THE HUNT**
Every day, a new target appears somewhere on the map. A compass points you toward it. Find it before anyone else. Climb the leaderboard. The Hunt turns passive watching into active exploration.

**FOUNDING CREATORS**
BEST launches with 100 founding creators — selected not by follower count, but by independent BEST score data. Every founding creator gets a permanent spot on the map. This is not a popularity contest. It is a meritocracy backed by data.

**YOUR DATA, YOUR RANKING**
BEST never sells your viewing habits to advertisers. Your Discovery Score belongs to you. Your Marks are yours. The ranking algorithm serves the audience, not the platform.

No platform has ever offered this. That is why BEST exists.
