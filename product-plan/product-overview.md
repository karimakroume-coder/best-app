# BEST — Product Overview

## Summary

BEST is a global independent video ranking platform that answers one question no platform answers: what is genuinely the best content on earth right now — independently, without algorithmic bias or platform conflict of interest.

## Planned Sections

1. **Spatial Map (World Best)** — The core experience — a navigable spatial map of ranked video cards with zoom levels (1x1, 3x3, 5x5, WORLD BEST formation), DROP animation, and the BEST Score algorithm powering all rankings.
2. **The Mark System** — Color + word cultural expression — liquid spread animation, strip keyboard for word selection, html2canvas snapshot, and permanent mark on video cards.
3. **Mandala Control Center & Five Map System** — Hidden UI that blooms from a rotating gold mandala, plus navigation between the five maps with cinematic camera transitions.
4. **Hunt Game & Discovery Score** — Daily discovery challenge with compass navigation, gold pulse on discovery, and the Discovery Score system.
5. **Creator Studio & Fireflag System** — Creator control over Map 2 permanent coordinates and the scarcity-based Fireflag endorsement system.

## Product Entities

- **Video** — A YouTube video tracked by BEST with ranking, score, and permanent coordinate
- **User** — A player with Discovery Score, badges, and personal collection
- **Mark** — A permanent color + word declaration left on a video card
- **Fireflag** — A scarcity-based endorsement (10/week, 20 max active)
- **Hunt** — A daily target video and player attempts
- **Coordinate** — A permanent spot on Map 2 owned by creators

## Design System

**Colors:**
- Primary: Champagne Gold `#F0C040` — rank numbers, icons, borders, active states
- Secondary: Bordeaux `#5C1A1A` — card backgrounds, rich surfaces
- Neutral: Warm near-black `#0D0800` — primary background
- Text: Parchment `#F5E6C8` — all text, cream surfaces
- Emboss: `#D4A017` / `#B8860B` / `#8B4513` — layered gold letterpress

**Typography:**
- Display: Times New Roman Bold — logo wordmark, championship headlines
- UI: Bebas Neue — buttons, labels, rank numbers, category strips
- Script: Pacifico — AI poetic descriptions, soft accents

## Implementation Sequence

Build this product in milestones:

1. **Shell** — Set up design tokens and the mandala-first application shell
2. **Spatial Map (World Best)** — Core single-card video view with embossed rank, metrics, and mandala
3. **The Mark System** — Color + word declaration with liquid spread and snapshot
4. **Mandala Control Center & Five Map System** — Full bloom UI and map navigation
5. **Hunt Game & Discovery Score** — Daily challenge and reward system
6. **Creator Studio & Fireflag System** — Creator controls and scarcity endorsements

Each milestone has a dedicated instruction document in `product-plan/instructions/`.
